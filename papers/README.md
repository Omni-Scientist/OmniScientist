# Sample papers

Five papers written end to end by OmniScientist, selected for their peer-review scores across a diverse set of
evidence modalities. Each was produced by a single one-click run (`--stage run`) on the bundled case named below,
then scored on the 0 to 10 rubric by the cross-family judge panel (`deepseek-v4-flash` and `gemini-2.5-flash-lite`),
with the Sonnet 5 backbone. Every number in each paper traces to real code output.

| Paper | Discipline | Evidence | Composite | Overall | MM-grounding |
|---|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| [Cramér-Rao scaling of exponent precision in maximum-likelihood recovery of monomial Feynman laws](physics_feynman_symbolic_regression.pdf) | Physics | Formula / table | **7.1** | 7.0 | 3.5 |
| [Sequential versus bursty leaf initiation distinguishes maize from tomato in 3-D scans](plant_science_3d_phenotyping.pdf) | Plant science | 3-D point cloud | **7.2** | 6.5 | 7.5 |
| [A continuum-removed absorption-depth index tracks residue and tillage-intensity ordering in corn fields](remote_sensing_hyperspectral.pdf) | Remote sensing | Hyperspectral image | **6.3** | 6.0 | 5.0 |
| [Transient-impulsivity features trained on slider units show reduced performance drop on valve units](machinery_acoustic_diagnostics.pdf) | Machinery | Audio | **7.1** | 7.0 | 7.0 |
| [Coherent polarized signals in a substantial fraction of noise-labeled STEAD traces](seismology_stead_noise.pdf) | Seismology | 3-component signal | **6.9** | 7.0 | 4.5 |

*Composite* is the mean of the seven peer-review dimensions; *Overall* is the holistic score; *MM-grounding* is the
multimodal-grounding dimension, the one a text-only system cannot address. These are candidate papers from a research
system, each with its own Limitations section; they are shown as representative output, not as peer-reviewed results.
